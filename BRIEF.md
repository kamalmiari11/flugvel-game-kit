## Sound

The device has a passive buzzer: short square-wave beeps, one at a time, no
music. The player can mute it, so nothing may depend on it.

**What should make a noise:**

> *e.g. "a blip per point, a falling buzz on losing, a rising three-note
> flourish on a new best". Saying nothing here gets you the default, which is
> roughly that.*
